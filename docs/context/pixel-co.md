# Pixel & Co. (web design business, @pixelandco.design)
Read owner.md first.

## What we sell
Websites for small Saudi businesses that have Instagram but no website. Free sample first, then paid. Rough price 1,000–2,500 SAR (confirm with the dad).

## Outreach method
- Find small accounts (200–5,000 followers, posting recently, no website in bio, Saudi city or 05 / +966 number).
- Skip: big or verified brands, anyone with a good website already, other countries (Iran, Oman, Malaysia got mixed in before).
- DM in Arabic plus English. Never say "I already made your site" unless it's actually made. Offer: "I can make you a free sample".
- Messages land in Message Requests: follow, like and comment first, use the WhatsApp number in the bio, follow up after 2–3 days. 20 DMs a day.
- Claude can't browse Instagram. The owner sends screenshots and Claude judges them and writes the DM.
