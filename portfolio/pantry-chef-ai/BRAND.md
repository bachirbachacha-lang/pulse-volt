# Brand: Potwise

Everything here is original work made for this project. `reference-style.png` was used for direction only (a friendly phone recipe app with a chef helper). The name, colours, fonts, logo and pictures are different from it on purpose: the reference is yellow and green with a robot mascot and photos; Potwise is paprika red and cream with a pot mark and drawn plates.

## Name

**Potwise**. "Wise about what goes in the pot."

- Tagline: **Cook what you already have.**
- How to write it: one word, capital P only. Not "PotWise", not "Pot Wise".
- The helper inside the app is called **the chef**. It has no human name and no robot face.
- Name check: one quick web search on 14 Nov 2026 found no recipe app with this name. That is not a trademark check. The owner should check properly before any public use.

Other names I considered, if you want to change it (decision D1 in PLAN.md): Ladlewise, Cupboard Cook, Sprig and Spoon.

## Voice

Warm, short, practical. Like a friend who cooks well.

- Say: "You have 5 of the 7 ingredients." Not: "Ingredient match score: 71%."
- Say: "This is a sample suggestion." Never pretend the sample chef is real AI.
- No guilt about food, no health promises.

## Type

| Role | Font | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Headings, recipe names | **Fraunces** (600) | A soft serif with character. Feels like a cookbook, not a tech product. |
| Everything else | **DM Sans** (400, 500, 700) | Clean and easy to read at small sizes on a phone. |

Both are free under the SIL Open Font Licence and are installed with the app from the `@fontsource` packages, so nothing loads from another company's servers.

Sizes: 28 (screen title), 22 (section title), 17 (card title), 15 (body), 13 (labels). Minimum tap target 44 px.

## Shape and feel

- Rounded corners: 20 px on cards, 14 px on inputs, full pill on buttons and tags.
- Soft, short shadows only on raised cards.
- Food pictures are drawn plates (a circle plate with coloured shapes), generated by code from each recipe's own colours. No photos.
- Icons are simple line icons drawn for this project.

## Logo

Files: `brand/logo-mark.svg` (the app icon) and `brand/logo-full.svg` (icon plus name).

The mark is a cooking pot with a small smile and a four-point spark rising from it where steam would be. Pot = the food you have. Spark = the idea of what to cook.

Rules:

- Keep clear space around it equal to the height of the spark.
- Smallest size: 24 px for the mark.
- Do not recolour, stretch, or put it on a busy picture.
- On paprika backgrounds use the cream pot without the rounded square.

Note: the word "Potwise" in `logo-full.svg` is live text. It shows in Fraunces where that font is installed and in a normal serif elsewhere. Turning it into fixed outlines is a small job for later.
