Garlic Butter Pasta

Silky pasta with slow-cooked garlic, butter and a squeeze of lemon. Ready before the table is set.

20minutes
Easylevel
2servings
520kcal est.

Ingredients

You have 5 of 7
  • Spaghetti (in your pantry)200 g
  • Garlic (in your pantry)4 cloves
  • Butter (in your pantry)40 g
  • Olive oil (in your pantry)1 tbsp
  • Lemon1/2
  • Parsleysmall handful
  • Salt (in your pantry)to taste

Steps

  1. 1

    Boil the pasta in salted water until just tender. Keep a cup of the cooking water.

  2. 2

    Melt the butter with the oil on low heat. Add sliced garlic and cook gently until soft and golden.

  3. 3

    Toss the pasta in the garlic butter with a splash of the cooking water until glossy.

  4. 4

    Finish with lemon juice and chopped parsley. Serve straight away.

Calories are a rough estimate for one serving.
