Creamy Garlic Pasta

Ribbons of pasta in a garlic cream sauce with parmesan, topped with sliced pan-seared chicken.

25minutes
2servings
690kcal est.

Ingredients

You have 4 of 8
  • Fettuccine200 g
  • Chicken breast1 large
  • Garlic (in your pantry)4 cloves
  • Cooking cream200 ml
  • Parmesan40 g, grated
  • Butter (in your pantry)20 g
  • Parsleysmall handful
  • Salt (in your pantry)to taste

Steps

Tap a step when it is done
  1. 1

    Boil the pasta in salted water until just tender. Keep a cup of the cooking water.

  2. 2

    Season the chicken and cook it in half the butter until golden and cooked through. Rest it, then slice.

  3. 3

    Cook the garlic gently in the rest of the butter, pour in the cream and simmer for 2 minutes.

  4. 4

    Stir in the parmesan and the pasta until glossy. Top with the chicken and parsley.

Calories are a rough estimate for one serving.
