1 · AI

AI is your worker, not your boss

AI builds, writes and designs fast. You still decide, take action, and put your name behind it.

What AI does well

Websites, ad videos, captions, Arabic and English copy, translations, product research, customer replies, working out profit.

What AI can't do for you

Post on your accounts, pay for ads, sign contracts, verify with Nafath, or be the face of the brand.

  • Clear input, better output. Screenshots and exact details (price, colour, country) get you the right answer on the first try.
  • Never fake results. No AI "before and after" hair, no fake reviews, no fake follower counts. Platforms ban it, and customers stop trusting you.
  • Check what matters. Prices, delivery times and health claims should always be true. AI can make mistakes too.
2 · Money

Know your numbers

Profit = Sale price − (product + shipping + fees + ads)

  • Revenue isn't profit. "10,000 SAR in sales" can still be a loss after ads.
  • Start small, test, then grow only what works.
  • Reinvest early profits into ads and better products before spending on yourself.
  • Keep business money separate from personal money. One account for the store.
  • Nobody gets rich in a week. Most "7,000 sales in a week" videos sell courses.

4 · Business

What makes people buy and come back

  • People buy results and feelings. A calm scalp and fuller-looking hair, not "50 mL of oil".
  • Trust sells. A clean site, honest delivery times (7–14 business days), real reviews, fast replies, easy refunds.
  • Upsells and bundles raise every order. That's why the brush sits on the oil page and 2–3 bottle deals exist.
  • Focus. One hero product done well beats ten random ones. Add products that fit the same customer.
  • Do it properly. A licence, honest claims (never "cures hair loss"), and SFDA registration once you keep stock in Saudi Arabia.
  • Automate later. At 5–10 orders, set up DSers so AliExpress orders and tracking happen in one click.
5 · Ads

Free posts build you. Paid ads feed you.

Free, every day

Spotlight, TikTok, Reels

1 video a day, 8–11pm Saudi time, trending sound, 3–4 hashtags. Switch between products freely.

Paid, after payments work

Snapchat first

50 SAR a day, 2–3 videos in one ad, the oil only, 3–4 days in a row before judging. Then TikTok.

  • The first 2 seconds decide everything. Open with the hook, not the logo.
  • Real beats perfect. Your hands, your bathroom, the real bottle.
  • Test, keep, kill, scale. Keep the video that sells, stop the rest, raise budget slowly on the winner.
  • Don't switch products daily in paid ads. Each switch restarts the learning.
  • Spy for free: facebook.com/ads/library and TikTok Creative Center → Top Ads. Long-running ads are the profitable ones.
  • Never buy followers. Fake followers kill your reach. Use the in-app "Promote" button instead.
6 · Investing

If you make 100,000 SAR a year

Can you make more? Yes. A store that makes 100,000 SAR in a year can grow past that with more winning ads and products. But investing comes after the business is safe, in this order:

  1. Keep the business running. Enough cash for ads, stock and refunds for the next 2–3 months.
  2. Emergency savings. 3–6 months of your personal costs, kept in cash.
  3. Reinvest in the store. Early on, the business usually earns more on each riyal than any stock does.
  4. Then invest 10–20% of profit (for example 10,000–20,000 SAR) for the long term.
Most of the investing money

Diversified, long-term funds

Low-cost funds that hold many companies, bought monthly and held for years. Sharia-compliant funds are widely available in Saudi Arabia.

Only a small slice

Crypto

Very volatile. At most about 5% of what you invest, and only money you can afford to lose completely.

Avoid

Traps

Day trading, borrowed money (leverage), "signals" groups, and anyone promising guaranteed returns.

  • Age: investment accounts in Saudi Arabia usually need you to be 18+. Until then, your dad would hold it for you.
  • Zakat: savings you hold for a full year are subject to zakat (2.5%). Saudi Arabia has no personal income tax.
  • Ask a professional (a licensed advisor or your bank) before investing large amounts. This page is general guidance, not financial advice.
7 · Mindset

Stay hungry, not cocky

  • Most people quit after 5 videos. Post 30 days in a row and you're already ahead.
  • Failures are data. A bad ad tells you what not to do next.
  • Don't compare yourself to screenshots on TikTok.
  • Protect your money and time from get-rich-quick courses and apps.
  • Rest when you're tired. Everything saves. The store will still be there tomorrow.
