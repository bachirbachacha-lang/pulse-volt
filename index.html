Questions

What's the difference between PULSE Energy and PULSE Sparkling?

PULSE Energy (Arctic Rush) is a zero-sugar energy drink in a 500 mL can, built for energy, hydration and focus. PULSE Sparkling is sparkling water with real fruit in 330 mL cans, caffeine free with no added sugar.

Does PULSE Sparkling contain caffeine?

No. All four sparkling flavors (Citrus, Mango, Berry and Watermelon) are caffeine free.

Is there sugar in PULSE?

Arctic Rush has zero sugar. The sparkling range has no added sugar and uses natural flavors.
