Questions

What's the difference between PULSE Energy and PULSE Sparkling?

PULSE Energy comes in five zero-sugar flavors in 500 mL cans, built for energy, hydration and focus. PULSE Sparkling is sparkling water with real fruit in 330 mL cans, caffeine free with no added sugar.

Does PULSE Sparkling contain caffeine?

No. All four sparkling flavors (Citrus, Mango, Berry and Watermelon) are caffeine free.

Is there sugar in PULSE?

Every PULSE Energy flavor has zero sugar. PULSE Sparkling has no added sugar; the 2 to 3 g per can comes naturally from the real fruit juice.

How much caffeine is in PULSE Energy?

Every PULSE Energy flavor has 160 mg of caffeine per 500 mL can, about the same as one and a half cups of coffee. We recommend no more than 2 cans a day. It isn't suitable for children, pregnant or breastfeeding women, or anyone sensitive to caffeine.

How much does PULSE cost?

PULSE Energy is $2.99 a can, $10.99 for a 4-pack or $29.99 for a 12-pack. PULSE Sparkling is $1.79 a can, $9.49 for a 6-pack or $17.99 for a 12-pack. Shipping is free on orders over $40.
